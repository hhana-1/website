Scroll to spin the star

The star turns half a revolution for every screen you scroll, and eases into place when you stop.

↓ keep scrolling

One element, one script

Add an element with data-star-scroll, include star-scroll.js as a module, and keep star.glb next to it.

Tweak it with attributes

data-turns-per-screen, data-axis, data-float, data-twinkle, data-pearl.

Light on the page

It only renders while it is on screen and the tab is visible, and it respects “reduce motion”.
